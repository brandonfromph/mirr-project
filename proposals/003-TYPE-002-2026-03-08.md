## Summary

Add `Signed(u32)` as a third `SignalType` variant, enabling `i1`..`i64` signal
declarations in MIRR source. This unlocks two's complement arithmetic, arithmetic
right shift, signed comparisons, and correct signed wire/register emission in
SystemVerilog and FIRRTL.

## Design Decisions

- D1: No implicit signed↔unsigned conversion.
- D2: Unary negation as `UnaryOp::Negate`. `-42` is `Negate(Integer(42))`.
  Negate on Unsigned(N) → Signed(N+1). Negate on Signed(N) → Signed(N).
- D3: `>>` on Signed operands = arithmetic right shift (emitted as `>>>`).
- D4: No cross-category comparisons (Signed vs Unsigned → E605/E606).
- D5: No new error codes — reuses E601-E607.
- D6: No Bool ↔ Signed(1) promotion.
