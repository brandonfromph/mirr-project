## Motivation

The `docs/` directory contains 53 tracked files. Of those, 29 are internal project management artifacts, unimplemented feature specs, meeting agendas, blank templates, stub APIs, or brainstorming notes that the public should not see. These files:

1. **Clutter the public repo** — external users see internal task checklists and "TBD" owners
2. **Make the project look unfinished** — stub docs like `mcp_api.md` say "to be filled in"
3. **Expose internal process noise** — meeting agendas, hazard analysis drafts, RTM with "Not verified"
4. **Contain stale/wrong content** — `mcp_roadmap.md` has raw AI-generated brainstorming appended
5. **Document features that don't exist** — `src/interpreter/` does not exist in the codebase

Additionally, the paper has one remaining contradiction at line 701.

The Jekyll `_config.yml` already excludes all 29 files from the published site, confirming the project's own intent to keep them internal.
