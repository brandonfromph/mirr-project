## Summary

Companion Rocq (Coq) project that formally proves correctness properties of
the MIRR width inference solver. 15 theorems covering termination, monotonicity,
soundness of all constraint rules, least-fixpoint correctness, SCC classification,
and truncation detection.

## Philosophy Gate

- **Generative power of three.** No language changes. PASS.
- **NASA P10.** Formal verification strengthens P10 compliance. PASS.
- **Hardware-synthesizable.** Proofs verify the analysis pass, not the language. PASS.
- **Properties don't affect hardware.** Rocq proofs are build-time verification only. PASS.

## Rocq Module Structure

```

```
